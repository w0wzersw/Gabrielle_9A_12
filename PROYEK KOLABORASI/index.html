ABOUT GOCHUCHAE (hover over !)

Gochuchae is small shop focusing on the making and selling of products including kimchi, formed by highschoolers as a way to get money for needs. Kimchi is a traditional Korean food, made with vegetables and through a fermentation process. Our small shop also hire events about teaching others on how to make their own kimchi with our fresh, high quality, and nutricious ingredients. Our small business welcomes every customer to try out our kimchi which possesses high in nutrition and a sharp, tangy, and delicious flavor to taste.

OUR EVENT !

We not only just serve products of kimchi like we mentioned. We also teach others about how to make kimchi with alternative ingredients! Not every kimchi needs to follow the same recipe from many different sources, we have everything prepared from the ingredients to the instructions! If you, the person reading, would like to join our event on learning how to make kimchi, feel free to contact us! We don't bite ^_^ !
